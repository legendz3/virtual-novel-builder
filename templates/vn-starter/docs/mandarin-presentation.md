# Mandarin presentation and speech

This is the authoring policy for new or revised Mandarin content across chapters. Every Chinese occurrence must have a deliberate display policy and a separate speech decision, including ordinary dialogue, narration, messages, props, choices, exercises, feedback, and vocabulary review. The reusable runtime contracts below implement these requirements for character dialogue, vocabulary entries and phrase tiles; other Chinese occurrences retain their existing document/phone/choice representations and still require deliberate planning. Preserve approved existing content outside a scoped revision.

**Current speech coverage:** thirteen chapters contain 2,912 events and 384 recording definitions: 131 vocabulary and 253 dialogue/phrase/announcement entries. All 61 new files are integrated and [approved by the author after listening](../production/mandarin-contracts/revisions/spoken-audio-one-seven/audio/user-listening-approval.json), with technical acceptance in the [revision status](../production/mandarin-contracts/revisions/spoken-audio-one-seven/status.json). `MandarinDialogue` in `src/game/learning/mandarin.types.ts` and `mandarinSchema.ts` defines the current supported modes:

| Mode             | Required authored support                                           | Initial display                                                             | Speech and resolution                                                                                                                                              |
| ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `supported`      | Every exact Chinese segment, tone-marked pinyin and English meaning | Complete support, including inline or mixed English/Chinese dialogue        | Matching `recorded` speech is required for spoken Chinese, including fully supported corrections. Only an explicitly reviewed mixed explanation may be `unvoiced`. |
| `meaning-hidden` | The same complete segment data plus `reveal.eventIds` and purpose   | Chinese visible; listening pinyin opt-in, otherwise visible; English absent | Matching recorded character speech is mandatory; all outcome paths reach declared full support.                                                                    |
| `audio-only`     | The same complete segment data plus reveal policy                   | No transcript by default; accepted listening button reveals pinyin only     | Matching recorded character speech is mandatory; all outcome paths reach declared full support.                                                                    |

`layout: "block"` requires one segment equal to the entire dialogue text. `layout: "segments"` preserves original prose and supplies support for each exact Chinese occurrence; already complete inline support need not be duplicated. Plain text cannot evade the contract: `chapterSchema.ts` detects Han characters and verifies complete, non-overlapping segment coverage, including supplementary Han and emoji offsets. Optional legacy pinyin/translation/audio/display fields are accepted only when they agree with canonical content. More granular withholding than these three modes needs an approved capability change rather than invented fields.

`mandarinSpeech.ts` applies the speech contract to the source dialogue itself. `mandarinSpeechIssue(text, content)` rejects silent standalone Chinese and any Chinese-bearing dialogue without a recording or an explicit contextual exception. An unvoiced exception requires supported segments layout, `reason: "mixed-explanation"` and a nonblank `purpose`; it is reserved for genuine metalinguistic prose, not a performed Chinese model with an English gloss. Contextual review remains necessary; adding Latin characters cannot silently exempt a line.

A mixed recorded line uses optional `mandarin.speech.spokenText` with segments layout to specify exactly the Chinese that is spoken. Every authored Chinese segment must be covered in source order. Validation removes only punctuation and whitespace before comparing the complete lexical sequence; omitted, reordered or added words and pinyin/English in the projection fail. `mandarinSpokenText(text, speech)` supplies this exact recording identity, falling back to the whole source text when no projection is needed. A block utterance may include a spoken proper name such as Emma and still use its exact whole-line recording; this is distinct from pinyin or English glosses in mixed segments layout. The projection changes neither displayed prose nor support/reveal timing. The [accepted seven-chapter supplement](../production/mandarin-contracts/revisions/spoken-audio-one-seven/gameplay-supplement.json) contains thirteen exact projections and three reviewed explanation exceptions; these event lists are preservation evidence, not a permanent schema bypass.

`chapterMandarinValidation.ts` checks exact speech utterance/speaker identity and reveal destinations. Every resolution path must reach a declared supported host. Retry cycles may continue when they have a supported exit; terminating branches and closed cycles without one are rejected. Listening panels inherit one identical immediate recorded dialogue on every incoming route, with only narration allowed between it and the panel. A different intervening dialogue, ambiguous route or earlier reuse of the same clip cannot supply its mode. A correct-only reveal is insufficient when a wrong answer can end without support.

`mandarinSupport` is the current optional source-bound support contract on narration, dialogue and ordinary choice. The old `languageReview` field is rejected and its panel/renderer removed. Four `natural-meaning` hosts declare an existing text/prompt span that occurs exactly once; source Hanzi/pinyin appears immediately above the complete English text flow, without a second canonical translation or a grid inside the sentence. The exact meaning span remains ordinary inline text, preserving punctuation and paragraph order. At the original support-placement checkpoint, eight `question-context` hosts placed a compact silent quotation above the reply/prompt. The source's authored `characterId` supplies attribution, including GUY before his introduction; its question is never presented as the current speaker's line. Natural support cannot share a host with a competing canonical Mandarin body. Source text uses normal style/weight and independent responsive rem sizes: at least 18px Hanzi, 16px pinyin/meaning and 14px attribution at the default root size. Explicit null and implicit final-array conditional exits are terminal branches for reveal, placement and deduplication validation.

Each binding needs a same-chapter reduced dialogue source, reciprocal reveal destination and explicit purpose. `resolved-answer` must identify that source's assessment and its immediate correct/incorrect destination; a retry hint has no resolved wrong destination. `recognition`/`clarification` names the exact contiguous intervening events, with no assessment, choice, phrase-builder or chapter-end interruption. No fuzzy English matching or generic already-understood flag certifies support. Semantic relevance still requires Writer/Director review; graph reachability alone is insufficient.

The optional `alreadySupportedOutcome` on question context requires both one exact committed wrong option and transient evidence that this chapter session displayed and successfully advanced its current fully supported repeat. Every wrong-route path must cross that repeat and reach the shared host; bypass/terminal/closed-cycle routes fail. At Saturday's shared response, exact `tonight-free` history plus exact current-session repeat evidence omits the duplicate. Saved history alone never does: reload/direct resume at the merge, or after the repeat but before the merge, displays full context. Resuming at the repeat and advancing it normally can establish evidence. Correct, absent and unknown answers always display context. No cursor/completion inference or new save flag is used. Source/repeat audio retains its existing playback; contextual support adds no recording control or audio calls. Mute/pause/audio failure does not determine text support. Evidence clears on Begin/replay, chapter switch, save-provider reinitialization and hook remount; it never enters save data. See the [legacy-resume correction](../production/mandarin-contracts/revisions/review-placement/merge-review-implementation.md) for the accepted compatibility boundary and current verification.

## Display rules

For optional conversational choices that teach usage, tone, or a near miss, follow [Optional language detours](language-detours.md). Full translation may remain visible when the question is conversational fit rather than translation recall; all display, reveal, and required-dialogue-audio rules below still apply.

**Default: show Chinese characters, tone-marked pinyin, and a natural English meaning together.** Use this for ordinary dialogue and first exposure to new language outside an exercise. A nearby authored English explanation can supply the meaning without duplicating it, if its correspondence is clear. Use consistent ordering, styling, and controls across chapters.

Support may be reduced for a stated story or learning purpose. Director must record what is visible initially, what is withheld, and exactly when or how it is revealed. Omitted content fields, available audio files, or differences between components must not accidentally decide the presentation.

| Context                                  | Chinese characters                   | Pinyin                                                                  | English meaning                                                                           | Reveal rule                                                                                                                                           |
| ---------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ordinary dialogue / new language         | Visible                              | Visible                                                                 | Visible                                                                                   | Full support by default, whether voiced or unvoiced.                                                                                                  |
| Familiar-language recall / immersion     | Visible                              | May be withheld                                                         | May be withheld                                                                           | Cite earlier exposure and provide the planned support on request or in a defined follow-up beat.                                                      |
| Guided listening                         | Visible                              | Visible by default; omit only for a stated objective                    | Withheld if understanding the meaning is the question                                     | Reveal full support after the answer is resolved.                                                                                                     |
| Listening without text support           | Withheld                             | Withheld                                                                | Withheld                                                                                  | Reveal the transcript, pinyin, and meaning after the answer is resolved.                                                                              |
| Reading / meaning inference              | Visible                              | Specify according to whether character reading is being tested          | Withheld when it would give away the answer                                               | Reveal full support after resolution; do not auto-play a pronunciation cue that defeats the stated reading goal.                                      |
| Phrase construction / response selection | Show authored Chinese where relevant | Required on every tile; specify option support consistently             | Allow support that serves construction; withhold support that answers the stated question | Document bank/placed-tile and choice/feedback behavior; provide the completed phrase with full support after resolution.                              |
| Written messages / environmental text    | Preserve the authored Chinese        | Provide in accessible UI alongside the text or through a planned reveal | Provide in accessible UI alongside the text or through a planned reveal                   | Use full support for first exposure unless a defined reading/inference task requires delay. Avoid baking optional support or answer cues into images. |
| Vocabulary / completed-lesson review     | Visible                              | Visible                                                                 | Visible                                                                                   | Full support and matching pronunciation recording are required by the vocabulary contract.                                                            |

**Supported reading must still give the player a reason to interpret Mandarin.** Before accepting a reading or meaning-choice interaction, check whether English alone supplies its answer across the entire lead-in and question, including document rows, narration, accessible labels, images and audio. Keep useful pinyin when the goal is meaning inference, and reveal answer-bearing English after resolution. A fully translated document can serve as a study or story passage; a multiple-choice restatement of its visible English does not become meaningful practice because it is labeled guided reading or awards zero XP.

For retryable exercises, “resolved” means the accepted answer or another authored completion route; a wrong attempt can give coaching without exposing the answer before retry. For one-attempt exercises, either answer resolves the exercise. Both success and failure completion routes must reach the planned explanation. State any intentional delay precisely.

Do not assume a player knows a phrase solely because it appeared in an optional earlier branch or a skipped chapter. A reduced-support plan must account for direct chapter entry and branch-dependent exposure. Support reveals must have consistent effects: a meaning lookup or replay does not silently become Help, grant XP, or change the grade. Any assistance rule belongs in the gameplay specification and must be verified across chapters.

## Speech rules

**Every standalone Chinese character utterance requires audio in the speaking character's assigned voice, with or without a comprehension game.** This includes single words, phrases and complete sentences. Determine the spoken language from the character's utterance, independently of the learning support: displayed pinyin, an English meaning, or a later English reveal never makes the recording optional. For example, Li Wei saying `平时。` requires his recording even while “Píngshí — Usually; as a rule” is visible. Director must map every such beat, including optional branches and spoken corrections, to a matching approved recording or a required new clip in the exact assigned voice. For new generation, apply the model in [voice-cast.md](../story/voice-cast.md#generation-model-assignments); approved reuse retains its actual historical provenance. Choose automatic or on-demand playback; unvoiced is not an option for these lines. This dialogue rule does not require voicing written props, silent reading activities, or other non-dialogue text. Preserve historical content outside the requested revision scope.

Inline formatting is not an exemption: when a character models or reads back Chinese alongside pinyin or an English gloss in the same text field, the spoken Chinese still requires a matching recording. Distinguish that support from an actual mixed-language explanation; preserve the authored text and explicitly bind the spoken portion.

Plan speech separately from the display/reveal policy, subject to required audio for standalone Chinese character utterances and listening exercises:

- **Unvoiced:** deliberate text-only presentation, appropriate for silent reading, internal narration, or explicitly planned mixed English/Chinese explanation. It still follows the display rules and cannot be used for a standalone Chinese character utterance merely because its English meaning is displayed.
- **Automatic playback on beat entry:** use a planned recording when hearing the line supports pronunciation, conversational immersion, tone, or an emotional moment. Provide the established replay control and respect mute/pause and browser playback restrictions.
- **On-demand playback:** use a planned recording when the player chooses to hear a word, phrase, message, or review entry. It must remain distinct from selecting an answer, editing a tile, or submitting an exercise.

Listening exercises, standalone Chinese character utterances, vocabulary entries and phrase tiles require recordings. Other Chinese content may also be voiced for a clear learning or narrative purpose; no comprehension minigame is required. The Director must decide and document voiced coverage, including deliberate unvoiced groups where permitted. A character having a cast voice does not mean every line must be recorded. Recording a line does not justify removing its pinyin or translation.

Use the **official MiniMax MCP** for new planned character recordings and the exact assignments/settings in [voice-cast.md](../story/voice-cast.md). This policy permits purposeful spoken Chinese outside comprehension games within an authorized chapter task. The coordinator accepts the clip plan through normal planning; no additional per-line request or approval is needed. Reuse matching approved clips, and preserve existing recordings and deliberately unvoiced lines outside the revision scope. Music and SFX are separate.

For a voiced line, record the exact spoken text, speaker, purpose, playback/replay timing, and audio ID; the production audio plan supplies cast, paths, and provenance. Resolve missing cast or generation capability rather than substituting another voice/provider. Apply character-specific Mandarin settings to all new Mandarin clips, including non-exercise dialogue. Do not imply that displaying pinyin means it is spoken: recordings contain the intended Chinese utterance unless a bilingual teaching line is explicitly authored.

When speech is unavailable or muted, ordinary dialogue retains its planned text support. A listening exercise must offer the established playback/unmute path or an explicitly designed text-supported alternative; do not silently reveal its answer, change the teaching mode, or treat a playback failure as a wrong answer. Plan and verify accessibility and reveal timing together: withheld assessment cues must also be absent from accessible labels, tooltips, images, and transcripts until their reveal point; ordinary supported text and permitted reveals must be accessible.

Record the purpose, initial characters/pinyin/English visibility, reveal trigger and destination, speech mode and reason, speaker/audio plan reference, and comparable prior usage. Inventory every standalone Chinese dialogue beat across the whole chapter and all branches; verify a required clip and correct speaker independently of support mode. QA must derive its coverage from runtime utterances as well as the clip plan, so an omitted or unvoiced plan entry cannot hide a missing recording. Specify the same decisions in affected gameplay entries. New chapter number alone is not a reason to change a convention.

Developer maps the accepted plan to actual content fields and shared components. Reuse supported options; identify and implement any needed reusable capability within the authorized chapter scope before claiming the plan works. Planning labels in this document do not create runtime enums, reveal buttons, or playback modes automatically. Report discrepancies rather than silently dropping pinyin/translation or adding audio because a file exists. Graphic Designer follows the specified text/support visibility for props and images and never embeds withheld cues.

QA adds `Mandarin presentation and speech` evidence to `qa-report.md`: check every Chinese occurrence against the default/exception mapping, including non-game dialogue and vocabulary; compare equivalent uses across chapters; verify actual on-screen and accessible text, support alignment, reveal paths, selected playback mode, voice/provenance, and audio lifecycle. Test applicable answer/retry, pause/mute, direct-entry, save/resume, and replay paths. Use listened-to evidence for pronunciation and voice identity; disclose inability to ingest audio and attribute any user listening confirmation to the exact files. File existence or successful decoding alone is not a listening check.

Unexplained support omissions, accidental answer leaks, unplanned voiced/unvoiced differences, missing required recordings, or unverified required behavior prevent acceptance. A standalone Chinese character utterance without a matching, playable recording in the assigned character voice is a defect even if the clip was omitted from the plan. Update this policy's supported examples and affected technical guides during [mandatory chapter documentation](../production/README.md#mandatory-chapter-documentation), preserving the distinction between intended policy and verified runtime behavior.
