# Site favicon

The favicon uses **心 (xīn, “heart”)** from the title logo in ivory on coral. Its four substantial strokes retain a Chinese identity at browser-tab size. It was generated with the built-in image-generation tool, using `public/assets/ui/beijing-heartbeat-title.png` as a brand reference. The full title logo and existing game artwork are unchanged.

Browser assets are `public/favicon-32.png` (32×32), `public/favicon.ico` (32×32 ICO), and `public/apple-touch-icon.png` (180×180). `index.html` links them. The ICO also supports browsers requesting the conventional root path. The image was reduced and format-converted using macOS `sips`; no visual elements were added during packaging.

The character is a compact brand mark, not an instructional utterance, so the tiny icon carries no pinyin or audio. Its pronunciation and meaning are documented above. The original generated image remains under `output/favicon/beijing-heartbeat-xin-source.png` as a local design artifact.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production website favicon for Beijing Heartbeat, a romantic visual novel for learning Mandarin Chinese.
Generate one square favicon artwork, 1024x1024, flat front view, opaque background, no device frame or presentation canvas.
Reference image: the existing game's title logo. Use its Chinese character 心 as the primary identity, simplifying only its typographic style for tiny favicon legibility. This new favicon must visibly communicate Chinese, not look like a generic medical heart icon.
Design: exactly one Chinese character 心 centered on a flat muted coral-red square background (#ae483e). The character is warm ivory (#fff3df). Use an elegant but very bold, well-formed Chinese calligraphic/serif character with the correct four strokes of 心, balanced proportions and generous separation between the strokes. Its shape must be unmistakably and correctly 心, not 必, not a heart pictogram, not a Latin letter. The main curved hook should be substantial and graceful; three separate dot strokes should remain large enough to survive at 16 or 32 pixels. The character occupies about 76–80% of the square width, centered optically in the square. Smooth crisp antialiased edges. Two flat colors only. No other Chinese characters, no pinyin, no English, no initials, no heart silhouette, no heartbeat zigzag, no airplane, no temple, no tiny flourishes, no stroke labels, no gradients, texture, shadows, glow, highlights or border. A Chinese literary romantic brand mark, simple and legible at tiny sizes.
Text, exactly: 心
