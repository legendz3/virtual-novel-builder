### Listening attempt and support variants

- **One-attempt consequence:** Chapter One, Chapter Three, Chapter Four, Chapter Six, Chapter Seven, Chapter Eight, Chapter Nine, Chapter Ten and Chapter Eleven resolve an answer into an authored success/correction branch. A wrong response completes the exercise rather than silently becoming retry-until-correct. Reward amounts belong to the specific content; Chapter Three’s time check and both Chapter Four checks and Chapter Six’s changed-location check award zero XP.
- **Retry until correct:** Chapter Two’s `kai-question-check` uses `retryHint`. A miss preserves the question and hint state without completing the exercise. A correct answer unlocks Emma’s personal response choice.
- **Text-supported listening:** Chapter Four’s Sofia and Chapter Six’s changed-location question show Chinese and pinyin, withholding upfront English meaning. Chapter Six restarts the clip after intervening unpaid-concourse narration; each wrong option has its own authored movement and reply before the full correction.
- **Audio-only listening:** Chapter Four’s Yichen question and panel omit transcript, pinyin and translation before resolution. The panel restarts the recording after intervening narration; this differs from adjacent question/panel beats that share continuous playback.

Preserve approved variants. New or revised support must follow [Mandarin presentation](mandarin-presentation.md), with an explicit objective and reveal rule. Do not copy accidental omissions from older content.

The [Chapter One/Four listening repair](../production/listening-repair/implementation-report.md) keeps these variants unchanged. Both Chapter One mistakes now receive full Chinese/pinyin/English support at `interesting` after three short teasing beats; correct A bypasses that deliberately unvoiced correction. Sofia's entire opening preserves the action-order question until Mandarin supplies it. Regression checks therefore start before the lead-in and inspect both outcomes, not just the assessment panel. No extra attempt, help state, reward or component was introduced.

## Shared rules for reuse

1. Give the interaction a story purpose and a learning objective. Usually one to three significant interactions per chapter is enough; ordinary supported Chinese and deliberate recordings can add practice without another game.
2. Follow [Mandarin presentation](mandarin-presentation.md): full characters, tone-marked pinyin and English support by default; every reduction needs a purpose and reveal rule. Check the full lead-in, images, accessibility text and audio for answer leaks.
3. Preserve shared controls, feedback, help/retry semantics and audio lifecycle. Meaning lookup and pronunciation do not silently become penalties or assistance. Player intention and language correctness are separate decisions.
4. Specify every outcome, branch merge and reward, including intentional zero effects for ungraded learning or passive actions. Personal romance options within authored/revised scope need meaningful relationship consequences for every option, including respect for boundaries. Commit awards once. Existing chapter rewards are authored facts, not a universal scoring formula.
5. Resume restores relevant choices/drafts without duplicate awards. Explicit replay or beginning a different chapter resets the destination chapter’s recorded choices, completion, drafts and rewards; learned vocabulary and other chapters remain. Visiting a chapter link alone does not reset a save.
6. Keep state and grading in React/business modules; Phaser renders. Test applicable success, error, help, audio, accessible display, save/resume/replay and earlier equivalent interactions.

See the required [gameplay consistency process](../production/README.md#gameplay-consistency). Planned differences in difficulty, teaching modes and outcomes need an explanation, not an unrelated implementation fork.
